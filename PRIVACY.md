# Privacy Policy for sleek

This document contains the types of information that is collected by sleek and how I as its developer use it.

I myself am very sensitive about my own privacy and in these days of massive and needless collection of personal data, I want sleek to be different. I am not interested in who sleek's users are and will therefore not gather any sensitive data.

However, I want to know how many users are actively using sleek and, in case a user explicitly agrees to it, how sleek is being used. The app communicates with a Matomo instance I host myself. The data is exclusively sent via a secure SSL-encrypted connection. sleek follows a standard procedure of logging. The information collected by Matomo includes only masked internet protocol (IP) addresses, operating system, screen resolution, date and time stamp, app version, and distribution channel. Also, a non-personalized random user id is generated once during installation and persists for the lifetime of the application. This way, I can see how many unique sleek users are out there without collecting sensitive data.

**Analytics are disabled by default.** When you first launch sleek, you'll see a welcome screen that explains analytics and lets you choose whether to enable them. Your choice is completely up to you, and you can change your decision anytime in the Settings window. If you choose not to enable analytics, that's perfectly fine — sleek works beautifully either way. Existing users who upgraded to a version with analytics will have it disabled by default, and the welcome screen will appear when they next open the app.

The analysis can be disabled in the settings window at any time. For first-time users, no data is sent to Matomo until you explicitly opt in. For existing users, analytics remain off until you choose to enable them.

## Event logging

Events are mostly clicks on buttons, checkboxes, links, menu items, and interactions with UI elements like the todo list, filters, and navigation. This information is valuable to me, as it tells me which features are used and which ones are not. Those insights help me keeping sleek clean of unnecessary features that will interfere with sleek's usability. This type of logging will not collect any personal information like the content of your todos, filters, or any text you type. It will only tell me which elements have been clicked.

## Cookies

sleek itself doesn't use cookies. Data that needs to be persisted is written to a local file that does not transfer anywhere else.

Analytics are optional and disabled by default. Enabling analytics indicates your consent to this Privacy Policy. You can change this setting anytime without affecting sleek's functionality.

If you have additional questions or require more information about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact me.
